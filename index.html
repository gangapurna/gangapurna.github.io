About Me

I’m a Budapest-based security systems engineer with over 20 years of experience working on integrated security systems in Hungary and abroad – video surveillance (CCTV), access control, intrusion detection and the security IT networks behind them. I focus on practical solutions, clear communication and continuous learning. I’m particularly interested in how generative AI and data analysis can be applied in my field. Outside work, I enjoy travelling, spending time outdoors and time with my family.
